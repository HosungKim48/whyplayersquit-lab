Story & writing critics leave most often

54% stopped, against 38% of all critics. 3% of negative reviews. Largest group above average: gameplay & design choices (15% of reviews, 40% stopped). Loud but staying: performance (29% of reviews, 34% stopped).

78% of under-2-hour reviews are negative. Steam refunds are available up to 2 hours of play.

Win back

6 · Player voice (paraphrased)

Short paraphrases that show the kind of complaint in each group. They are not quotes, and no review text is published.

Performance

A five-year-old game that stutters, has a cramped field of view, awful save design, and tedious movement.
The developers only fixed performance so they could sell new paid DLC.

Crashes & bugs

Five hours in with repeated crashes; not recommended at this price, so wait for patches and a sale.
Terrible save system and repetitive side quests; improves after twenty hours, but still feels incomplete and inhumane.

Gameplay & design choices

Harsh design choices: awkward controls, bad save system, goblins everywhere, and anti-cheat crashes in a single-player game.
NPCs force unwanted conversations whenever you walk nearby, happening constantly.

Price & monetization

Performance is poor, and the single save slot means changing your character costs real money; unacceptable.
Don't buy: DRM, microtransactions in a seventy-dollar single-player game, and terrible optimization.

Not enough content

Inconvenient design and poor optimization; it feels rushed, and the main story ended abruptly after about forty hours.
Combat is fine but the game feels unfinished and poorly performing, with no fixes after months; felt scammed.

7 · Checks by team

Show the checks for each team

Reviews show where to look. Each lead below can be confirmed or rejected with internal telemetry.

EngineeringPerformance29% of negative reviews▼ Stay more often

34% of these reviewers walked away; median 4.0 hours played at review.
  • Compare session length and next-day return by hardware tier in your own telemetry; reviews cannot see hardware.
  • Publish a performance target per spec tier and report against it in patch notes, so waiting players know when to return.

Engineering & QACrashes & bugs15% of negative reviews▼ Stay more often

27% of these reviewers walked away; median 6.9 hours played at review.
  • Rank crash signatures and save-loss bugs by how many players hit them in their first sessions, not by ticket count.
  • Check whether players who crashed in session one or two ever started a third session.

Game designGameplay & design choices15% of negative reviews▲ Leave more often

40% of these reviewers walked away; median 19.6 hours played at review.
  • Separate 'I dislike this mechanic' from 'this mechanic is unclear': the first is a design call, the second is onboarding.
  • Test optional rule settings or modes for the most-named mechanic and measure retention of players who switch them on.

Publishing & pricingPrice & monetization11% of negative reviews● About average

39% of these reviewers walked away; median 3.9 hours played at review.
  • Compare refund and early-exit rates across editions, regions and price points; price complaints concentrate before much play.
  • Check whether paid add-ons land while core complaints are still open; the spike list shows review timing around such posts.

ProductionNot enough content10% of negative reviews▲ Leave more often

43% of these reviewers walked away; median 33.5 hours played at review.
  • Find the hour mark where these reviews cluster and look at what players have run out of by then.
  • State on the store page what is in the game today versus planned, so expectations match the build.

8 · Limits & data

Show limits and data files
  • Only people who wrote a review; they are a small and unusual part of the player base.
  • Playtime after a review is Steam's public figure at the snapshot (2026-11-24). Offline play and other platforms are not counted. Reviews newer than 30 days are left out of walk-away rates.
  • Editing a review may refresh its "playtime at review" figure. Without edited reviews the walk-away rate of negative reviewers is 41% (with them: 38%).
  • Reason labels come from a language model reading up to 1,200 characters per review in its original language. In a second-model check of 170 random labels across languages, 92% of main reasons were judged correct and 93% of quit-statement tags; expect roughly that error rate in reason shares.
  • "Walked away" is set at under 1 more hour. At under 2 hours the negative-reviewer rate is 41% and 10 of 10 complaint groups keep the same verdict; at under 5 hours it is 48% and 10 of 10 keep it (rank correlation with the 1-hour ordering: 1.00 and 0.99).
  • Groups that complain about different things also differ in hours played, purchase timing and expectations; the regression in section 1 controls for some of this, not all.
