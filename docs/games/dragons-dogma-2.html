Quit-signal report · Action RPG · released 2024-03-22 · data snapshot 2026-11-24

Dragon's Dogma 2

Who left after reviewing, when they gave up, and which complaints go with actually leaving — from every public English Steam review.

Bottom line
  • 31% of the 44,682 English reviews are negative. The median negative review was written after 6.9 hours of play (positive: 22.7 hours).
  • A negative review is not the same as quitting. 41% of negative reviewers logged less than 1 more hour after posting; the other 59% kept playing, and 44% played 10 or more further hours. Among positive reviewers, 8% stopped that quickly.
  • The most common main complaints are performance (29%), price & monetization (15%), crashes & bugs (14%), out of 13,012 labeled negative reviews.
  • Compared with all negative reviewers (41%), reviewers walked away clearly more often after complaints about story & writing (55%), interface & controls (51%), not enough content (47%); and clearly less often after complaints about online, accounts & drm (22%), crashes & bugs (31%), performance (36%). These are associations, not causes.
  • The first two hours are the sharpest edge: 76% of reviews written with under 2 hours played are negative, and 58% of those reviewers did not come back for another hour.
  • What players say and do differ: of reviewers who wrote that they had quit, 44% still logged another hour or more. Of those who wrote they would wait for fixes, 53% went on to play 10+ more hours.

6. What a studio could do with this

Reviews point at where to look; a studio's own telemetry can confirm or reject each lead. Suggested checks for this game's largest complaint groups:

Performance29% of labeled negative reviews
36% of these reviewers walked away (lower vs. all negative reviewers); median 4.0 hours played at review.
  • Compare session length and return rate by hardware tier in your own telemetry; reviews cannot see hardware.
  • Publish the performance target per spec tier and report progress against it in patch notes, so waiting players know when to come back.
Price & monetization15% of labeled negative reviews
39% of these reviewers walked away (similar vs. all negative reviewers); median 2.9 hours played at review.
  • Compare refund and early-exit rates across editions and price points; price complaints concentrate before much play.
  • Check whether paid add-ons land while core complaints are still open; review timing around DLC posts is shown in the spike table.
Crashes & bugs14% of labeled negative reviews
31% of these reviewers walked away (lower vs. all negative reviewers); median 7.0 hours played at review.
  • Rank crash signatures and save-loss bugs by how many players hit them in their first sessions, not by ticket count.
  • Check whether players who crashed in session one or two ever started a third session.
Gameplay & design choices14% of labeled negative reviews
46% of these reviewers walked away (higher vs. all negative reviewers); median 18.8 hours played at review.
  • Separate 'I don't like this mechanic' from 'this mechanic is unclear': the first is a design choice, the second is onboarding.
  • Test optional rule settings or modes for the most-named mechanic, and measure retention of players who switch them on.
Three uses of this report
  • Prioritize by behaviour, not volume. Put a complaint group higher when its authors walk away more often than other negative reviewers (section 2, right-hand chart).
  • Protect the riskiest hours. Section 1 shows where negative reviews and walk-aways concentrate; that is the stretch of the game to instrument and playtest first.
  • Talk to the "waiting" group. Section 3 sizes the reviewers who said they would return after fixes and shows how many did; patch notes aimed at their stated reasons are the natural test.

Limits of this report

  • Only English reviews. Only people who wrote a review; they are a small and unusual part of the player base.
  • Playtime after a review is Steam's public figure at the snapshot date (2026-11-24). Offline play and play on other platforms are not counted. Reviews newer than 30 days are excluded from walk-away rates.
  • If a reviewer edited their review, Steam may have refreshed the "playtime at review" figure. Without edited reviews the walk-away rate of negative reviewers is 44% (with them: 41%).
  • Reason labels come from a language model reading up to 1,200 characters per review, one main reason each. In a hand check of 60 random labels, 88% of main reasons were judged correct and 95% of quit-statement tags; expect roughly that error rate in reason shares.
  • Associations only. Players who complain about different things also differ in other ways (hours played, when they bought, what they expected).
