Good to know

Which games work?

Any paid Steam game with public reviews. Free-to-play games don't work yet: Steam's review data for them is unreliable.

Is a monthly plan worth it for my game?

It fits games that still get new reviews every week. For an older, quieter game, a one-time Custom report is the better buy.

Where does the data come from?

Only public Steam reviews. No review text or reviewer IDs are published, and nothing needs access to your accounts.

What's in a Custom report?

Everything on a dashboard, plus complaint topics from every negative review and a breakdown by review language. See one.
