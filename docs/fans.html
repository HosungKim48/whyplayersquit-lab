When fans review

When players who recommend a game write their review. 300,346 recommended reviews, six games.

When

Half of all recommendations come between 12 and 81 hours

Median: 33 hours. Critics: 30 hours.

Where in their playtime

Heavier players recommend earlier in their playtime

Under 10 h in total: 73% of the way through. 500 h+: 27%.

Review and leave

34% of the lightest players recommend as they stop

Among players who total 500 h+: 7%.
